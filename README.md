# My Poker Journey
## Overview

I started playing online cash game in 2020. Since then, I have played over half a million hands across public platforms and private clubs, and now I focus more on live games. I have decent results with valid records, with a  win rate of about 7BB/100 (big blinds per one hundred hands) on GGPOKER, and 18BB/100 in private online clubs.

I have strong understandings in modern poker theory. I use GTOWizard to study general optimal strategies, and PioSolver "node lock" to construct exploitative strategies. I also conduct large-scale hand-history analysis to develop strategies to exploit systematic deviations from equilibrium.

Bankroll management has always been the most important part of my journey. I level down when I reach predetermined stop loss, and I level up only after achieving statiscally significant results over a meaningful sample size. The discipline allows me to manage variance without ever going broke.

I have also been part of the best poker community in Taiwan, with the best coaches and friends. We regularly review hands, form study groups, hold seminars etc. This environment has played a critical role in my development as a winning player.
